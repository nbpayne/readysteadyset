What We Do

We make studio shooting accessible for productions of every size.

Ready Steady Set provides professional modular film set flats—1.2m wide, 2.4m high—that click together to form any room your script calls for. Bedrooms, living rooms, offices, corridors, diners. You name it, we can build it.

Paint them. Wallpaper them. Dress them. Reconfigure them between setups. And when the shoot wraps, hand them back – knowing they'll go out on their next production, not into landfill.
