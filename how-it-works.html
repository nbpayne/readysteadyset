The Process

From brief to set, ready to shoot.

Our modular flats are engineered for the way productions actually work – fast to configure, infinitely customisable, and back in your hands by wrap.

The Flat

1.2 metres wide. 2.4 metres high. Endlessly configurable.

Each flat is a precision-built timber-framed panel – the industry-standard building block of theatrical and film set construction. Ours are built to last, not for a single use. That means sturdy enough for repeated reconfiguration, and finished to take any surface treatment your art department has in mind.

Bolt them together side by side for a straight wall. Create corners. Build an L-shaped room. Add a window flat or a door flat. The combination possibilities are virtually unlimited.

Step by Step

From enquiry to set, here's how it works.

01

Tell us your brief

Share your script requirements, the rooms you need to recreate, your studio space dimensions, and your shoot dates. The more detail, the better the recommendation we can give you.

02

We design your flat configuration

Based on your brief, we'll recommend the number of flats, the optimal arrangement, and any specialist panels (windows, doors) you'll need. We'll also suggest a colour strategy if you'd like us to pre-paint.

03

We deliver to your studio

We deliver the flats to your studio within Sydney metro for $250 each way. Everything arrives ready to assemble – along with any hardware you need to connect them. Allow a few hours for your art department to configure and dress the set.

04

Shoot. Reconfigure. Shoot again.

The magic of modular: halfway through your shoot, redress the set completely. Rearrange the flats. Change the room. Add a wall. Remove one. Your production can have multiple distinct spaces within the same hire.

05

Hollywood wrap – we collect

Call wrap. Strike the set. We collect the flats and they return to our inventory, ready for the next production. Zero landfill. Zero waste. Just a clean, efficient end to your shoot.

Why Studio?

Why the best productions move out of locations and into studios.

Total lighting control

Top light a scene. Create soft light from a "window" that doesn't actually face the sun. Hold the perfect golden hour from 9am to 5pm. In a studio set, you control every photon.

Shoot more, faster

Hollywood wrap at 6pm. Come back the next morning with everything exactly where you left it. No travel time. No set-up from scratch. Your producer's budget will thank you.

No location headaches

No permits. No irate neighbours. No ambient noise from passing trucks. No losing the location because the owner changed their mind. Your set is yours for the duration of the hire.

Shoot day for night

Your script calls for a night scene? Block the studio, light through a window, and shoot it at 11am. Your crew goes home at a reasonable hour. Your director gets the scene exactly right.

Multiple rooms, one hire

Configure a living room on Monday, move four flats on Tuesday and you have a corridor. The modular system means your set budget covers multiple scripted spaces – not just one.

Genuinely sustainable

Each flat that goes out on hire displaces a custom-built flat that would otherwise be binned. Over dozens of hires, the material savings are enormous – and we're proud of that.
